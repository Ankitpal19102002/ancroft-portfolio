Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

Yes! HealthFirst Clinic is actively welcoming new patients of all ages. Simply book an appointment online or call our office and our team will get you registered within minutes.
We accept most major insurance plans including Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and Medicaid. Please call us if you're unsure about your specific plan — we're happy to verify your coverage before your visit.
After booking a telehealth appointment, you'll receive an email with a secure video link. No app download is required — just click the link on any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera. Telehealth visits are available evenings and weekends.
Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications (or the bottles themselves), any relevant medical records or lab results, and a list of questions you'd like to discuss. Arrive 10–15 minutes early to complete intake paperwork.
Routine and wellness appointments are typically available within 3–5 business days. For sick or urgent visits, we reserve same-day or next-day slots. Telehealth appointments often have the shortest wait times.
